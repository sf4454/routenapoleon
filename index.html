The Route Napoléon

The Route Napoleon is one of the most famous driving roads in the south of France. Starting in Cannes and ending in Grenoble, this 325km stretch of road runs through the western foothills of the French Alps. The route passes through some of the most beautiful scenery in the region and the open road sweeping through the peaceful landscapes makes this the perfect road trip through southern France. The road bares the name of Napoleon Bonaparte, and follows a nearly identical route to the one he took after his escape from exile on the island of Elba.
